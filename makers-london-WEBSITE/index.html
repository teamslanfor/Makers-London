Meet the maker

From a home kitchen to Chelsea

Noor Freiha grew up in Lebanon in a family that believed in cooking from scratch and eating clean. A chemical engineering graduate from Columbia University, she taught herself to make chocolate at home.

Today, with Belgian chocolatier and pâtissier Alexandre Vandale, she makes the chocolate she always wanted to eat every day, in Chelsea, where she grew up.
