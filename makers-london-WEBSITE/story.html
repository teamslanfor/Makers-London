Our story

“I’ve loved chocolate my whole life. It’s always been part of my daily ritual.” Noor Freiha, founder

Where it began

Cooking from scratch, eating clean

Noor grew up in Lebanon, in a family that believed in cooking from scratch and eating clean.

A chemical engineering graduate from Columbia University and a former management consultant, she follows a mostly paleo way of eating, but never wanted to give up chocolate. So she taught herself to make it at home.

If you’re going to eat it every day, it should be guilt-free.

Noor Freiha Founder, Makers

guilt-
free
The chocolatier

Built from scratch

Belgian chocolatier and pâtissier Alexandre Vandale brings a classical background in luxury chocolate. He and Noor work closely on every recipe, building pralinés, caramels, ganaches, feuilletine, nougats and fruit jams from scratch.

The clean caramel alone took a month of daily work to perfect, because of one house rule:

If it does not taste better than the conventional version, it does not make the cut.

What’s never inside

Free from, full of flavour

No dairy, wheat, refined sugar, glucose or seed oils, and nothing artificial: no flavourings, colourings or preservatives.
