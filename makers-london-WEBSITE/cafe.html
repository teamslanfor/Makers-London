The shop

A little piece of Lebanon in Chelsea

The shop is inspired by the origin of cacao, with a maximalist palette, blue tones that nod to the west coast of Africa, and motifs from Noor’s childhood.

  • Hand-painted Delft tiles with the Lebanese flag, wild strawberries, coconut oil and palm trees: an ode to both Lebanon and LA
  • Handmade copperware from a Lebanese workshop
  • Space designed by Rosanna Bossom
  • Wallpaper by MINDTHEGAP, designed by Minnie Kemp
  • Banquette in Kit Kemp’s “Jogalong” for GP & J Baker
  • A mosaic floor spelling out the shop’s name
